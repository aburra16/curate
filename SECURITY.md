# Security Policy

Curate is an open-source explorer and editor for Decentralized Lists. This document is the target of the `Policy` field in the `security.txt` published at `curate.brainstorm.world`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Report privately through GitHub:**
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/NosFabrica/Brainstorm-UI/security/advisories/new)

Curate is run by the team behind Brainstorm, so reports about it go through Brainstorm's form. Please say in your report that it's about Curate.

Please do **not** open a public issue for a security report.

Include whatever you have: the affected page, the commit if you know it, reproduction steps, and what impact you think it has. A partial report is more useful than none.

### What to expect

We are a small team. We do not operate a paid bug bounty and cannot promise a fixed response window. We will acknowledge your report, tell you whether we consider it in scope, and let you know when a fix ships. If you would like credit, say so.
